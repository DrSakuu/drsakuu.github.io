# Philosophy

We are all one, give and receive love and love yourself. Love is the one thing that transcends space and time.

## Consciousness

We are all part of the same universe, but why do I only see things through my own eyes? In the beginning, there were only stones and plants that have no consciousness. Then came animals that have consciousness, and finally humans who are able to contemplate what consciousness is. Is it so that there is only one consciousness in the universe, but it is divided among beings? Consciousness is constantly growing apart. In humans, consciousness is constantly reaching a higher and higher level.

## The Kybalion

- [The Kybalion](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1363427.Kybalion)

The All that created the universe is eternal, cannot be subtracted from or added to. So why did it create our Universe? What could it possibly gain from it? That is the unknowable Absolute Truth. People who think they know it only know the Half-Truth and are destroyed. The final answer must be kept secret, do not let it consume your life. There is as yet insufficient data for a meaningful answer.

## The Trip

- [The Egg](https://youtu.be/h6fcK_fRYaI)

We are all on The Trip from birth to death, Big Bang to Heat Death of the Universe. Some people want to live fast and die young, some want to live slow and die whenever. Some people choose to be reincarnated as medieval peasants to see what it would feel like. You will be reincarnated as all your doctors and caretakers, your friends and enemies, so you should take good care of them too. The Universe lives for the longest time. You decide who you take along on your Trip.
